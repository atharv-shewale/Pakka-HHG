# Pakka — Submission Notes and Demo Script

## One-line pitch

**Pakka turns informal work promises into clear, reviewable agreements with a tamper-evident history — created through voice-first development.**

## Problem / who it is for / why it matters

Small freelance jobs and local services are often agreed through quick conversations. People later disagree about deliverables, price, deadlines, acceptance, or payment. Pakka helps freelancers, students, and small service providers capture the terms, identify missing details, record confirmations and updates, and produce a printable record for reference.

## What is meaningfully different

Pakka is not just voice transcription. It combines a clarification step before confirmation with a transparent event history whose SHA-256 links can be checked. The deliberate tamper test makes the integrity model visible during the demo. Its boundary is explicit: this is local, tamper-evident recordkeeping, not legal proof or escrow.

## 100-second demo script

**0:00–0:10 — Problem**  
“Small jobs often start with a quick promise. Later, the client and worker can remember the price, deadline, or deliverables differently. Pakka makes those terms explicit.”

**0:10–0:28 — Create the deal**  
Load a sample or speak/type: “I’ll design 20 posters for Rahul for ₹4,000 by Friday. Payment after approval.” Show the editable draft and the structured terms.

**0:28–0:40 — Clarify before confirming**  
Show a missing acceptance criterion or deadline warning. Complete the missing field and review the agreement. Emphasize that extraction hints are editable and never silently final.

**0:40–0:58 — Confirm and track**  
Activate the agreement, switch between the clearly labelled demo roles to confirm, add a short work check-in, and record a partial payment.

**0:58–1:15 — Proof File**  
Open the Proof File. Point to the agreed terms, timeline, amount remaining, and Verified status. Mention JSON export and print-to-PDF.

**1:15–1:30 — Integrity reveal**  
Click Tamper demo and run verification. Show Tampering detected. Say: “The stored record no longer matches its saved hash chain.”

**1:30–1:40 — Honest boundary**  
“Pakka is a local prototype. The role switch is a simulation, and the hash chain is tamper-evident recordkeeping—not legal enforceability, identity proof, blockchain, or escrow.”

## Recommended README first paragraph

Pakka helps freelancers and small service providers turn informal work promises into clear agreements and a tamper-evident event history. Create a draft, clarify missing terms, confirm the agreement, track work and payments, and export a printable Proof File. This local-first prototype does not process payments, verify identities, or provide legal guarantees.
